# Entrepreneur

**Run your business without the busywork.**

Entrepreneur is a simple, all-in-one CRM built for small business owners. Keep track of your customers, send professional quotes and invoices, and see how your business is doing — all in one place, with nothing complicated to learn.

## What it does

- **Customers & contacts** — everyone you do business with, organized and easy to find.
- **Quotes & invoices** — create professional documents in minutes, ready to send.
- **Reporting** — revenue, outstanding invoices, and activity, always at a glance.
- **Works offline** — your data lives on your own computer, not someone else's server.

## Free to start, with room to grow

The core of Entrepreneur is **free — no card required, no time limit.** Track customers, send invoices, and see your numbers, for as long as you need.

When you're ready for more, **Entrepreneur Pro** adds location intelligence (see your customers on a map) and deeper customer activity insights. Try it free for 14 days, no card required.
